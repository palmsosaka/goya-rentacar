About Goya Rent-a-Car

Goya Rent-a-Car has been renting cars in Okinawa since 2010 — 16 years. We operate a single main store in Senaga, Tomigusuku City, about 15 to 20 minutes from Naha Airport by our shuttle and two traffic lights from Senaga Island. We run our own airport shuttle, we tell you our coverage prices before you arrive rather than at the counter, and we prepare your paperwork in advance so you can leave as soon as you get the key. We are open every day, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

What We Stand For

Peace of mind, and no wasted time

Fill in your details online when you book and we will have your paperwork ready before you arrive, so there is nothing to write at the counter. Our shuttle runs on a set routine between the airport and the store, so you are not left waiting.

Clear, honest pricing

We publish our figures instead of hiding them. Optional coverage is ¥1,650 per day (CDW, which waives the ¥50,000 deductible) plus ¥350 per day (NOC coverage) — ¥2,000 per day, tax included. You know the total before you book, not after.
