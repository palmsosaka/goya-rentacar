Insurance & Coverage

Basic insurance is included with every car. Three optional coverage options let you reduce what you pay if something happens.

  1. TOP
  2. Insurance & Coverage

Basic Insurance (included with every car)

Every rental includes the insurance below. In the event of an accident, claims are paid up to these limits.

Personal injury (other people)Unlimited per person (includes Japan's compulsory automobile liability insurance)
Property damage (other people's property)Unlimited per accident (includes Japan's compulsory automobile liability insurance)
Vehicle damage (the rental car)Market value per accident, with a ¥50,000 deductible (excess)
Personal injury of driver & passengersUp to ¥10,000,000 per person

· The deductible (excess), and any loss exceeding the insured amount, is paid by the customer.
· No insurance is paid for accidents that fall under the policy's exclusions. Insurance may also not be paid if there is no police accident report, so please always call the police (110) after an accident.

Three Optional Coverage Options

These are optional — you decide whether to add them. The combination you choose determines how much you would pay out of your own pocket if something happens.

① CDW / Deductible Waiver
¥1,650 (tax included) / day
  • Waives the ¥50,000 deductible (excess) you would otherwise pay

If you book with an internet discount rate or a travel agency coupon, joining this option is a condition of receiving the discounted rate.

② NOC Coverage
¥350 (tax included) / day
  • Waives the NOC (Non-Operation Charge) explained below

Add this together with ① and the total is ¥2,000 per day. If an accident happens, you pay nothing at all — no deductible and no NOC (only when the other party involved can be identified).

③ Vehicle Damage Coverage
¥550 (tax included) / day
  • Applies to single-vehicle accidents (no other party) and hit-and-run damage where the other driver cannot be identified

If repair costs are within ¥50,000, the market-value amount is charged. Accidents caused by a traffic violation are not covered, and towing charges are not covered.

NOC (Non-Operation Charge) amounts

NOC is a charge used by rental car companies throughout Japan. If the car needs repair after an accident, it cannot be rented out during that time, and NOC covers part of that lost business. It is a fixed amount, regardless of how serious the damage is or how long the repair takes. These amounts are tax exempt. The same NOC applies to all vehicle classes. If you add ② NOC Coverage, you do not pay this at all.

What you pay, by combination

This is the part worth reading. Adding ① and ② together costs ¥2,000 a day and removes your out-of-pocket cost entirely.

What you add Per day What you pay if an accident happens
① CDW only ¥1,650 The ¥50,000 maximum deductible on property and vehicle damage is waived. You still pay the NOC.
① CDW + ② NOC Coverage ¥2,000 Both the ¥50,000 deductible and the NOC (up to ¥50,000) are waived. You pay nothing at all — but only when the other party involved can be identified.
① + ② + ③ Vehicle Damage Coverage ¥2,550 Also covers single-vehicle accidents and hit-and-run damage. Even then, the most you would pay is ¥50,000.

· ①②③ are tax included. NOC amounts are tax exempt.
· "Only when the other party involved can be identified" means: if you have a single-vehicle accident, or the other driver leaves the scene and cannot be found, ① and ② alone may not remove your cost. Option ③ is what covers those cases.

Key Terms Explained

Deductible (excess)

The amount you pay out of your own pocket after an accident, before insurance covers the rest. With us it is ¥50,000. Add ① CDW (¥1,650 / day, tax included) and you do not pay it.

NOC

Short for Non-Operation Charge. While the car is being repaired it cannot be rented out, and NOC covers part of that lost income. ¥20,000 if you can drive the car back to us, ¥50,000 if it has to be towed. Add ② NOC Coverage (¥350 / day, tax included) and you do not pay it.

Unlimited liability

Every car carries unlimited cover for injury to other people and damage to their property (including Japan's compulsory insurance). Injury to you and your passengers is covered up to ¥10,000,000 per person. Damage to the rental car itself is covered at market value with a ¥50,000 deductible.
