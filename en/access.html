Getting Here from Naha Airport

About 15–20 minutes

Our shop is roughly 15 to 20 minutes from Naha Airport by shuttle. We are in Senaga, Tomigusuku City — two sets of traffic lights from Senaga Island.

Free shuttle service

We run a free shuttle between our shop and Naha Airport, and between our shop and Akamine Station on the Yui Rail monorail — in both directions, so we also take you back when you return the car.
