Standard on Every Car

Non-smoking & sanitised

Every car in our fleet is non-smoking, and each one is treated with an air-catalyst sanitising process. You get in to a clean car, every time.

Car navigation & ETC included

A car navigation system and an ETC unit (the electronic toll device used on Japanese expressways) come as standard, at no extra cost.

* A small number of cars are not fitted with these.

Child seats available

Baby seats, child seats and junior seats are all available, so you can pick the right one for your child's age and size. Child seats are charged as an option.
