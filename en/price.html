Coverage — the second part of your bill

Basic insurance is already included in the daily rate. On top of that there are three optional coverage options. They are what decide how much you would pay out of your own pocket if something happened.

Option Per day (tax included) What it does
① CDW (Deductible Waiver) ¥1,650 Waives the ¥50,000 deductible — the amount you would otherwise pay yourself towards damage before insurance covers the rest.
② NOC Coverage ¥350 Waives the NOC (Non-Operation Charge) — the fee charged because a damaged car cannot be rented out while it is repaired. NOC is ¥20,000 if you can drive the car back to us, ¥50,000 if you cannot (tax exempt).
③ Vehicle Damage Coverage ¥550 Covers single-vehicle accidents (no other party) and hit-and-run damage where the other driver cannot be found.
What you add Per day What you pay if an accident happens
① only ¥1,650 The ¥50,000 deductible is waived. You still pay the NOC.
① + ② ¥2,000 Both the deductible and the NOC are waived. You pay nothing at all — only when the other party involved can be identified.
① + ② + ③ ¥2,550 Also covers single-vehicle accidents and hit-and-run damage. Even in those cases, the most you would pay is ¥50,000.

· ①②③ are tax included. NOC amounts are tax exempt.
· If you book on an internet discount rate or with a travel agency coupon, joining ① is a condition of that discounted rate.
· Full details on the Insurance & Coverage page.

Other fees you should know about

These only apply in specific situations, but we would rather you read about them here than hear about them at the counter.

Airport return: ¥5,500 (tax included)

Cars are normally returned to our store. If you are running late for your flight, you can return the car at Naha Airport instead for a ¥5,500 fee (tax included). Call us as soon as you know.

Return with a full tank

Please refuel before you return the car. If the tank is not full, we settle the difference based on how much fuel is left, in four bands (3/4 or more, half or more, 1/4 or more, under 1/4) and the tank capacity of the car.

Child seats (option)

Baby seats, child seats and junior seats are available and are charged as an option. Car navigation and ETC are standard and free. Ask us for the child seat rate when you book.

Payment is by credit card only. We cannot accept cash. If you are driving on an International Driving Permit, you must show both the IDP and your passport when you collect the car.
