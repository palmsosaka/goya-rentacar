Accepted Payment Methods

Credit card only

We accept the major international card brands. Payment is made at the shop when you pick up the car. Please make sure you have a usable credit card with you — without one, we cannot hand over the vehicle.

We cannot accept cash

Cash is not accepted, and neither are QR code payment services such as PayPay. Credit card is the only payment method available.

If you are driving on an International Driving Permit

Customers renting with an International Driving Permit are asked to pay by credit card as a rule. Please bring both your International Driving Permit and your passport.

Important Notes

A credit card is required — it is not optional

Credit card is our only payment method. Foreign-issued cards of the major international brands are accepted. If you arrive without a usable credit card, we will not be able to rent you a car.

What's Included in the Price

Your total includes the base rental fee, insurance plan, car navigation, and ETC device. We show you the total amount upfront — no hidden fees.
